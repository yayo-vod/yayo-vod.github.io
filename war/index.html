Accuracy audit

WarFilms.

Ranked by how closely each film follows what really happened. Names only. No plots. No spoilers. Stop.

How to read the grades

Grade A follows the record. Grade E only borrows a war as its setting. The order is a judgment from general knowledge of the films and the events, not a scholarly ranking, so expect to disagree about the middle.
